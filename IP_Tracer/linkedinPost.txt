Ever tried explaining to a non-tech friend what an IP address reveals? I built a bot that does the talking for you. 🕵️

After way too many evenings of wrestling with n8n's expression engine (yes, I'm looking at you, JSON.parse called 4,200 times), I'm proud to share @tracedip_bot — a Telegram bot that turns any IPv4 address into a full OSINT intelligence report.

Drop an IP, get back:
🌐 Network telemetry — ISP, ASN, hosting provider, connection type
📍 Geolocation — country, city, coordinates, hemisphere, map links
🕐 Time intelligence — timezone, DST, business hours, local period
💰 Currency & language data from the IP's region
⚖️ Risk scoring (0-100) with trust rating and hosting/residential classification
🌤 Optional weather & sunrise enrichment (because knowing the weather at 8.8.8.8 is critical intelligence apparently)

All of this runs on n8n with zero external runtimes — 10 nodes, regex-based routing, inline JSON.parse() expressions, and 170+ lines of bulk formatting JavaScript in a single Code node.

The 350-page research backing this covers:
• IPStack accuracy benchmarks (99% country, 97% cloud provider detection)
• Weighted additive risk engine design (TOR +50, Proxy +40, VPN +30...)
• Parallel enrichment branching saving 62% response time
• Cloud provider heuristics across 12 providers with 285 test samples
